The browser warned me the connection isn't private

That is expected, and it is safe here. Your Fire TV signs its own certificate, because no certificate authority on the internet will vouch for a private address like 192.168.x.x. Choose Advanced and then Proceed. Your browser remembers the choice.

The encryption itself is real — the warning is about who vouches for the key, not about whether the connection is encrypted. The TV shows the certificate's fingerprint under Help if you want to check it matches.

Nothing happens, or the page won't load at all
  • This device and the Fire TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network. A phone on mobile data cannot reach it.
  • Guest networks usually block devices from talking to each other (“AP isolation”). Use the main network.
  • Some 5 GHz and 2.4 GHz bands are separate networks on the same router. Try joining the one the Fire TV is on.
  • If the TV's address changed, reopen the address currently shown on the TV screen.
